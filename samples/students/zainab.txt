Bubble sort written in pseudocode for the DS lab.

procedure bubbleSort(list of items)
  repeat
    swapped = false
    for i from 1 to length(list) - 1 do
      if list[i - 1] > list[i] then
        swap list[i - 1] and list[i]
        swapped = true
      end if
    end for
  until not swapped

The outer loop runs at most n times and the inner loop compares neighbours,
so the worst case cost is n squared comparisons.
